The problem

Most businesses don’t need AI. They need four specific hours back.

The waste is rarely where people look for it. It is almost never in the decisions. It is in the retrieval, the re-typing and the chasing that surrounds them.

  1. 01Retrieval

    The answer already exists. Getting it takes two hours.

    Someone asks what last month’s figure was. An employee stops what they are doing, opens three systems, and comes back later with an estimate. The number was sitting there the whole time. The cost was the walk to it.

  2. 02Repetition

    Work that follows a fixed procedure, done by hand.

    Nobody ever wrote the procedure down, so it lives in someone’s head and scales only by hiring. If a task is the same shape every time, a person doing it is a person being wasted.

  3. 03The outsource tax

    Paying per unit, forever, for something buildable once.

    An agency charges per page, per listing, per report. The price is reasonable and the invoice never stops. The in-house version was never built because nobody had a spare fortnight.

  4. 04The phone

    Every unanswered call is a booking that went somewhere else.

    Front desks are busy at exactly the moments customers ring. The call that goes to voicemail at 4pm does not ring back.

What we won’t build

If you can buy it, don’t pay us to make it.

Reminders, chasers, review requests, rotas, booking portals, email autoresponders. Jobber, Fresha, Xero, Deputy and ManyChat already ship these as features, tested by thousands of businesses, for less than a day of our time. We will tell you that on the first call and you keep your money.

A build has to sit in one of five places
  • Unstructured input. Speech, documents, photographs, free text.
  • Judgement, not rules. The decision changes with context.
  • Data you own but cannot read. It’s in there, nobody can get it out.
  • An artefact that didn’t exist. The thing is written, not moved.
  • Compliance. It has to be provable, not just done.
Applied every time If your problem is not one of those five, the honest answer is software you can subscribe to this afternoon.

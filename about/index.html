About

A small studio, deliberately.

Al Kabbani Solutions is an AI and automation studio in the United Kingdom, led by Bashir Al Kabbani. We build custom systems for businesses that have a specific, expensive, repetitive problem, and we turn down the ones that don’t.

01Why small

There is no account layer here. The person who scopes the work is the person who builds it, which means nothing is lost in the handover and nobody is incentivised to sell you a larger project than the problem deserves.

The trade-off is real and worth stating: one build runs at a time. That is the reason each one gets finished, and it is also the reason there is occasionally a wait.

02Where it comes from

The background is data analysis. Years spent on the unglamorous end of it, where the job is getting a defensible number out of systems that were not designed to give you one, and then explaining why it is defensible.

That shapes what gets built here. The instinct is to measure the problem before proposing anything, to state assumptions as assumptions, and to be suspicious of any claim that arrives without a number attached, including our own.

How this shows up Every proposal names what it assumes and what it would cost if the assumption is wrong.
03How we write

Write like the surveyor, not like the vendor.

It is the rule for everything that leaves here, this website included. Report what is measurable. State the constraint and what it costs. No superlatives, no urgency, no claims that cannot be checked.

It is also why there are no logos on the work page and no testimonials anywhere. The businesses these systems run inside did not sign up to be marketing material, and a quote we wrote and asked someone to approve is not evidence of anything.

What is missing here No client logos. No testimonials. No headcount. Ask on a call and you will get the detail directly.

We would rather lose the project than build you something you can buy.
